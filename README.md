# My Favorite Movie: The Hows of Us 🎬

![Movie](https://img.shields.io/badge/Genre-Romantic%20Drama-red)

## About the Movie
**The Hows of Us** is a 2018 Filipino romantic drama film produced by *Star Cinema*. It follows a couple whose long relationship is tested by real-life struggles.

## Why I Love It
1. It feels realistic and relatable.
2. The chemistry of the lead actors is great.
3. It shows that love is not always easy.

## Highlights
- Directed by Cathy Garcia-Molina
- Released in 2018
- One of the biggest Filipino box office hits of its time

> "Love takes work, and the hows of it are what make it real."
